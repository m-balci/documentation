## Compare plans

Each plan is designed for a different kind of user.

| Plan | Price | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Free** | \$0.00/month | Exploring Webza with no cost |
| **Plus** (most popular) | \$19.99/month | Building and launching a single project |
| **Premium** | \$49.99/month | Unlimited projects with full power |

### Projects and AI credits

| Feature | Free | Plus | Premium |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How do I upgrade, downgrade, or cancel my plan?">
    To upgrade or downgrade, open a ticket from the **Support** page in the menu. To cancel, go to the **Billing** page and select **Cancel Subscription**. Your plan benefits stay active until the end of your current billing period. See [Change or cancel your subscription](/change-or-cancel-your-subscription).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do Free plan credits renew every month?">
    No. The Free plan includes 2M AI credits as a one-time grant. Plus and Premium include a new credit allocation every month.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I connect my own domain on the Free plan?">
    No. Custom domains are available on Plus (1 domain) and Premium (3 domains). On Free, you can publish to a custom `webza.ai` subdomain.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How many projects can I create?">
    Free and Plus include 1 project. Premium includes unlimited projects.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Where do I find my invoices?">
    See [Invoices and billing](/invoices-and-billing).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I get a refund?">
    Refunds follow the [Webza Terms of Service](https://webza.ai/terms).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
