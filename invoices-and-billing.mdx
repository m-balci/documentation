Open **Billing** from the sidebar to manage your subscription, monitor credit usage, and review past payments.

## What's on the Billing page

The Billing page is organized into four sections:

| Section | What you see |
| --- | --- |
| **Current Plan** | Your active plan, billing cycle, and next renewal date |
| **Build Credits** | Remaining credits and a history of credit usage |
| **Usage Stats** | Breakdown of credit consumption by project and AI model |
| **Transaction History** | Past payments, invoices, and credit pack purchases |

## Choose your first plan

If you do not have an active plan, click **Choose Plan**, select the plan that fits your needs, then choose a payment method and complete the purchase. Once payment is confirmed, your plan activates immediately and your credits are added to your account.

## Change your plan

To upgrade or downgrade your plan, open a Support ticket. The team will process the change for you.

<Note>
  Plan changes are not self-service. All members can open Support tickets.
</Note>

For more details, see [Change or cancel your subscription](/change-or-cancel-your-subscription).

## Cancel your subscription

To cancel, go to **Billing** → **Cancel Subscription**. Your access continues until the end of the current billing period.

Refund eligibility follows the terms at [https://webza.ai/terms](https://webza.ai/terms).

## Buy extra credits

If you run low on credits before your next renewal, you can purchase a one-off credit pack from **Billing** → **Usage** → **Buy Credits**. Packs are paid through the same method as your subscription and are added instantly. They do not renew automatically.

Learn more on the [Credits and usage](/credits-and-usage) page.

## View invoices and update payment details

Your **Transaction History** lists every payment and invoice. You can review amounts, dates, and statuses there.
