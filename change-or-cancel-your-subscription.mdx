You can cancel your Webza subscription yourself from the **Billing** page, and request an upgrade or downgrade by opening a ticket from the **Support** page. This page shows the steps for each change and explains what happens to your plan, credits, and published websites afterward.

<Info>
  **Looking for something else?** To compare plans, see [Subscription plans](/subscription-plans). For invoices and payment methods, see [Invoices and billing](/invoices-and-billing). For how credits work, see [Credits and usage](/credits-and-usage).
</Info>

## Upgrade or downgrade your plan

The Webza support team handles plan upgrades and downgrades for you. Open a support ticket and tell the team which plan you want to move to.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Support page">
    Select **Support** in the menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a ticket">
    Create a new ticket and include:

    - Your current plan.
    - The plan you want to move to.
    - Any questions about billing or credits for the new plan.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for confirmation">
    The support team reviews your request, applies the change, and replies to your ticket with the details.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  If you regularly run out of credits before your plan renews, upgrading to a higher plan is usually the simplest fix. See [Subscription plans](/subscription-plans) to compare credit allowances before opening a ticket.
</Tip>

## Cancel your subscription

Canceling stops future charges. Your plan benefits stay active until the end of your current billing period, and after that your account moves to the free plan.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Billing page">
    Select **Billing** in the menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Cancel Subscription">
    Select the red **Cancel Subscription** button.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the cancellation">
    A confirmation message appears: _"Are you sure you want to cancel your subscription? Your plan benefits will remain active until the end of your current billing period."_ Confirm to start the cancellation.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Your subscription continues until the end of the current billing period, so you can keep using your paid plan until then. You are not charged again after the period ends.

<Note>
  Changed your mind? Open a ticket from the **Support** page to move back to a paid plan.
</Note>

## What happens when you change or cancel

| Change | How to request it | When it takes effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Upgrade | Support ticket | When the support team applies the change and confirms it on your ticket. |
| Downgrade | Support ticket | When the support team applies the change and confirms it on your ticket. |
| Cancel | **Billing → Cancel Subscription** | End of the current billing period. Your plan benefits stay active until then, and your account then moves to the free plan. |

### Your unused credits

What happens to the credits you have not used yet depends on the change:

- **Downgrade to a lower paid plan:** Your unused monthly credits and rollover credits stay available until their original expiry date. Your new plan's monthly credit allocation starts from the next billing period.
- **Cancel (move to the free plan):** Your unused credits from the paid plan are **frozen**. You cannot use them on the free plan, and they are not refunded. If you move back to a paid plan before they expire, you can use them again until their original expiry date.

<Note>
  **Top-up and bonus credits are not affected by plan changes.** Top-up credits you purchased and any bonus credits you received stay usable after a downgrade or cancellation, including on the free plan, until they expire.
</Note>

For how credits expire and roll over, see [Credits and usage](/credits-and-usage).

### Your published websites

Your published websites stay live after you downgrade or cancel. Your projects remain in your account, and you can keep editing and publishing them on the free plan with your available credits.

<Warning>
  **Custom domains are not available on the free plan.** Custom domains are included in the Plus and Premium plans. If you cancel and your account moves to the free plan, your website stops loading on your custom domain and is available on its `webza.app` subdomain instead. See [Custom domains](/custom-domains).
</Warning>

If a feature you use is not included in your new plan, it stops being available when the change takes effect. See [Subscription plans](/subscription-plans) to check what each plan includes.

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How do I cancel my Webza subscription?">
    Go to **Billing** in the menu, select **Cancel Subscription**, and confirm. Your plan benefits stay active until the end of the current billing period. See [Cancel your subscription](#cancel-your-subscription).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I upgrade or downgrade my plan?">
    Open a ticket from the **Support** page and tell the team which plan you want. The support team applies the change and confirms it on your ticket. See [Upgrade or downgrade your plan](#upgrade-or-downgrade-your-plan).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do I lose access immediately when I cancel?">
    No. Your plan benefits stay active until the end of your current billing period. After that, your account moves to the free plan.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens to my unused credits if I cancel?">
    Your unused credits from the paid plan are frozen when your account moves to the free plan. You cannot use them on the free plan, and they are not refunded. If you move back to a paid plan before they expire, you can use them again until their original expiry date. Top-up credits you purchased and any bonus credits stay usable on the free plan until they expire. See [Your unused credits](#your-unused-credits).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do I get a refund when I cancel?">
    Refunds follow the refund terms in the [Webza Terms of Service](https://webza.ai/terms). Canceling stops future charges, and you keep your paid plan benefits until the end of the current billing period. If you believe you were charged in error, open a ticket from the **Support** page.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Will my websites go offline if I cancel?">
    No. Your published websites stay live on their `webza.app` subdomains, and your projects stay in your account. Custom domains are only available on the Plus and Premium plans, so a connected custom domain stops serving your website after your account moves to the free plan.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
