Webza offers support through tickets you open from the sidebar. You can also reach the team by email. This page covers how tickets work and what to expect.

## Open a new ticket

Click **Support** in the sidebar to view your ticket list. To create a new ticket, fill in the subject and describe your problem. Attach files if they help explain the issue. Screenshots of errors are especially useful for the support team.

## Ticket states

Each ticket has one of three states:

- **Open:** The support team is working on it.
- **Pending:** The team is waiting for a response from you.
- **Closed:** The issue is resolved.

If you reply to a closed ticket, it reopens automatically. You do not need to create a duplicate ticket.

## Privacy

Tickets are private. Only you and the Webza team can read them. Do not paste passwords or API keys into a ticket unless the team explicitly asks for them.

## Who can open tickets

All members on your account can open support tickets. Plus and Premium plan members receive priority support.

## Plan changes via tickets

You can request plan upgrades or downgrades through a support ticket. See [change or cancel your subscription](/change-or-cancel-your-subscription) for details.

## Contact by email

You can also email the support team directly at [**support@webza.ai**](mailto:support@webza.ai).

## Other ways to get help

- **Guided tour:** Restart the dashboard tour from the bottom of the sidebar. In the editor, click the circular-arrow button next to **Rebuild** to replay the editor tour.
- **Prompting tips:** Write clearer instructions to get better results from the AI. See [prompting tips](/help/prompting-tips).
- **Project chat:** Ask the AI for explanations or fixes directly in your project. See [project chat](/project-chat).
