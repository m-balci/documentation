Build your first website with Webza and put it live on the web in about ten minutes. There is nothing to install and nothing to configure: you describe what you want, Webza designs and builds it, and you publish it to a real address you can share. In this guide you build a website for **Golden Crust**, an imaginary neighborhood bakery, with a homepage, a menu with prices, and an order form. Swap in your own idea at any step; the process stays the same.

Building with Webza uses [credits](/credits-and-usage), and the free plan includes a one-time grant of 2 million AI credits. See [Subscription plans](/subscription-plans) for what each plan includes.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    [Sign up for free](https://webza.ai/register?utm_source=webza_docs&utm_medium=content) with email, Google, or GitHub. Webza opens your dashboard, ready to build. For login, password reset, and account settings, see [Create an account](/introduction/create-an-account).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe your website">
    The prompt box on the [dashboard](/introduction/dashboard-overview) is where every project starts. Describe the website you want in plain language.

    Try this prompt, or adapt it to your own idea:

    ```text Prompt wrap
    Build a website for Golden Crust, a neighborhood bakery. Include a warm hero section with a headline and an "Order online" button, a menu page with sourdough, croissants, and cinnamon rolls with prices, an About section with the bakery's story, and an order form where visitors enter their name, the items they want, and a pickup time. Use realistic sample content. Cozy, modern design. Works well on mobile.
    ```

    <Tip>
      Be specific about pages, sections, and the feel you want. The more context you give in the first prompt, the closer the first version will be to what you pictured.
    </Tip>

    <Accordion title="Use your own idea instead">
      Adapt one of these starters, or write your own prompt from scratch. The steps in this guide stay the same.

      ```text Portfolio wrap
      Build a portfolio website for a freelance photographer: a full-width gallery, an About page, a services and pricing section, and a contact form. Minimal, elegant design.
      ```

      ```text Local business wrap
      Build a website for a dental clinic: a homepage with services, a team section with photos, opening hours, a location section, and an appointment request form. Clean, trustworthy design.
      ```

      ```text Landing page wrap
      Build a landing page for a mobile app that helps people track their daily water intake: a hero with a download button, a features section, testimonials, and an FAQ. Fresh, bright design.
      ```
    </Accordion>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Watch the first version appear">
    Webza creates your project and starts building. The first version takes a few minutes.

    When it is ready, you work in the [editor](/editor): the [project chat](/project-chat), where you talk to Webza, sits next to the [preview](/preview), where your website runs live. Click around the preview, open the menu, and scroll through the sections.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make a change">
    Ask for one change at a time in the project chat and check the preview after each. For example:

    ```text Prompt wrap
    Add a "Today's specials" section above the menu that highlights two items with a short description and price.
    ```

    If the website works but does not look the way you pictured, say so in plain words. This is a perfectly good prompt:

    ```text Prompt wrap
    Make the whole site feel warmer and less corporate.
    ```

    You can also edit visually in the [editor](/editor), or open the [code editor](/code-editor) for full control over your site's files. Your changes stay private until you [publish](/publish).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check that it works">
    Before publishing, spend a few moments testing your website:

    - Click every button and link.
    - Fill in the order form to make sure every field works.
    - Check how the site looks on a phone. Most visitors will open your link on mobile.

    If something is off, tell Webza exactly what is wrong and where:

    ```text Prompt wrap
    On the menu page, the "Order online" button does nothing. It should scroll to the order form.
    ```
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish">
    Select **Publish** in the editor, alongside the other project actions. After a short deploy, your website is live on a free `webza.app` subdomain (for example `golden-crust.webza.app`), ready to share. See [Publish your site](/publish) for details, and [Custom domains](/custom-domains) to use your own domain instead.

    <Note>
      **Your live site is a snapshot.** Changes you make after publishing appear in the preview right away, but the live site only updates when you publish again.
    </Note>
  </Step>
</Steps>

That is the whole loop: describe, refine, publish. Every website you build with Webza, however ambitious, is this loop repeated.
