Your Webza account is your personal identity on Webza. You use it to log in, manage your profile and preferences, and access every project you build. You only need one account, no matter how many websites you create. Anyone with an email address can [sign up for free](https://webza.ai/register?utm_source=webza_docs&utm_medium=content) and start building right away. By creating an account, you agree to the [Terms of Service](https://webza.ai/terms).

## Sign up at webza.ai

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Webza">
    Go to [webza.ai/register](https://webza.ai/register?utm_source=webza_docs&utm_medium=content) to open the sign-up page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a sign-up method">
    Create your account with one of the available methods:

    - **Email and password**
    - **Google**
    - **GitHub**
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify your email">
    If you signed up with email and password, open the confirmation email from Webza and follow the link to activate your account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start building">
    After sign-up, Webza takes you to your dashboard. Describe the website you want in the prompt box to create your first project.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  New accounts start on the free plan, which includes a one-time grant of 2 million AI credits. See [Subscription plans](/subscription-plans) for what each plan includes and [Credits and usage](/credits-and-usage) for how credits work.
</Info>

## Log in to an existing account

If you already have a Webza account, go to [webza.ai](https://webza.ai) and select **Log in**. Use the same method you signed up with (email and password, Google, or GitHub). For example, if you created your account with GitHub, select **Continue with GitHub** instead of entering a password.

If you cannot log in, see [Reset your password](#reset-your-password).

## Access your account settings

Open your account settings to update your profile, preferences, and login details:

- From the dashboard menu, go to **Settings → Account**, or
- Go directly to [webza.ai/profile](https://webza.ai/profile)

See [Settings](/settings) for everything you can configure.

## Reset your password

If you signed up with **email and password**, reset it from the login page:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the login page">
    Go to [webza.ai](https://webza.ai) and select **Log in**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Request a reset link">
    Enter your account email and select **Forgot password?**
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set a new password">
    Open the reset email and follow the link to choose a new password.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If you signed up with **Google** or **GitHub**, there is no separate Webza password. Log in with the same provider, and reset that provider's password if needed through [Google Account recovery](https://accounts.google.com) or [GitHub password reset](https://github.com/password_reset).

<Tip>
  Not receiving the email? Check your spam folder and make sure you entered the address you signed up with.
</Tip>

## Account vs. project

Your account and your projects are separate:

- **Your account** is you. It holds your login, profile, preferences, and subscription.
- **A project** is a website you build. It holds your pages, chat history, drafts, and publishing settings.

One account can own many projects. Mark the ones you use most as favorites and find them quickly with [Find a project](/find-a-project).
