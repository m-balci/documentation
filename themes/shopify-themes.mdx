Generate a complete, installable Shopify Online Store 2.0 theme from a prompt. Download it as a `.zip` and upload it to your Shopify store.

<Note>
  Shopify themes are available on the Plus and Premium plans.
</Note>

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a Shopify theme project">
    Choose **Shopify Theme** as the output type and describe your theme.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build and refine">
    Chat with the AI to build and refine your theme.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Download the theme">
    Download the theme as a `.zip` file.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Install in Shopify">
    Upload the `.zip` in your Shopify admin and publish it when you're ready.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Create a Shopify theme project

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose the output type">
    On the dashboard, open the output selector next to the prompt and choose **Shopify Theme**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a template (optional)">
    Click **Customize** to pick a Shopify template, or leave it on **Automatic**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe your theme">
    Enter your prompt and click **Start**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The output type is set when you create the project and can't be changed later.

### Starting templates

Every Shopify project starts from a base theme. By default, this is **Skeleton**, Shopify's official minimal starter. It gives the AI a valid Online Store 2.0 structure with no built-in styles, so the design is written from scratch for your prompt.

If other Shopify templates are available on your plan, pick one under **Customize** to start from it. With the template picker on **Automatic**, the AI chooses from the Shopify templates available to you.

## Preview your theme

Shopify themes can't be previewed inside the Webza editor, because Shopify themes only render in a Shopify store. After a build, use **Download theme (.zip)** in the preview panel and install the theme in your store to see it.

<Tip>
  Upload the theme to your store without publishing it. You can then preview it from **Online Store → Themes** without changing your live storefront.
</Tip>

The panel does not show Undo, Redo, or Revision History buttons. Keyboard shortcuts still work:

- Undo: `Ctrl/Cmd + Z`
- Redo: `Ctrl/Cmd + Shift + Z` (or `Ctrl + Y` on some systems)

## Install the theme

<Steps>
  <Step title="Download the theme">
    In the Webza editor, click **Download theme (.zip)**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload to Shopify">
    In your Shopify admin, go to **Online Store → Themes → Add theme → Upload zip file**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Preview, customize, or publish">
    After upload, preview the theme, click **Customize** to edit it further in Shopify, or click **Publish** to make it live.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What theme projects can't do

A theme is installed in Shopify, not hosted by Webza, so some website features aren't available:

- **Publishing and custom domains**: the **Domains** tab is not shown.
- **GitHub and Supabase**: not available for themes.
- **Design mode**: the design system you choose at creation is applied at build time.
- **AI-generated images**: only available for website projects.
- **Analytics**: the analytics card is hidden. Add tracking in your Shopify store.

In the editor, the **Export** button is replaced by **Download theme (.zip)**.

## Changing plans

If you move from Plus or Premium to a plan without Shopify themes, you can still download your existing themes, but new builds on theme projects are blocked. Upgrade again to continue editing them.

## Duplicate a theme project

A duplicated Shopify theme project keeps the Shopify output type.
